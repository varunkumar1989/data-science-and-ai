## 90-day roadmap

| Weeks | Analyst track | Data science / ML track | GenAI track |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Excel lessons 1–6 | Python 1–10 | Python 1–12 |
| 3–4 | Excel 7–12 + SQL 1–5 | Python 15–22 (NumPy, pandas, EDA) | Python 15–19, 23–24 (APIs) |
| 5–6 | SQL 6–15 + SQL case study | Stats ML 1–6 (statistics, A/B) | GenAI 1–4 (LLMs, prompts, APIs, embeddings) |
| 7–8 | Power BI 1–6 + dashboard project | ML 7–15 (workflow, regression, metrics, CV) | GenAI 5–8 (RAG, tools, agents, MCP) |
| 9–10 | Python 1–16, 20–22 | ML 16–25 + one capstone | GenAI 9–12 + capstone app |
| 11–12 | AI analytics 1–4, portfolio polish | ML 26–28, portfolio polish | Portfolio, deployment, write-ups |
| 13 | Interview prep (Career 2) and applications | Interview prep (Career 3) | Interview prep (Career 3) |

## Job-search funnel

| Stage | Target per week | Tips |
|---|---|---|
| Research companies and roles | 15 | Match your projects to their domain |
| Referral / recruiter outreach | 10 | Personalised messages; alumni and community contacts |
| Applications | 10–15 | Tailor resume keywords; apply within 3 days of posting |
| Screens and tests | 2–4 | Track results; review mistakes |
| Interviews | 1–3 | Prepare company-specific cases |

Referral message template:

```text
Hi <Name>, I'm a data analyst (SQL, Power BI, Python) who built <project link> on <domain>.
I saw the <role> opening at <company> (job id). Could you refer me or share what the team values most?
Happy to send my one-page resume. Thank you!
```

Track everything in a simple sheet: company, role, date, source, status, next step, notes.

## Salary and offers

Research ranges on AmbitionBox, Glassdoor, LinkedIn Salary and peers for your city and experience. Ask for the full CTC breakdown (fixed, variable, joining bonus, benefits), negotiate on evidence (skills, competing offers, market data), and compare learning opportunities, team quality and growth, not only salary.

## First 90 days in the job

1. Learn the business: products, customers, key metrics and their definitions.
2. Find the data: sources, owners, known quality issues; document as you go.
3. Deliver a small, useful win in the first month.
4. Communicate: weekly updates, clear charts with insight titles, assumptions stated.
5. Automate repetitive reports; share reusable SQL and notebooks.
6. Keep learning: one new skill per quarter (dbt, cloud, experimentation, GenAI).

:::key
- Follow the 90-day roadmap with a weekly routine of learn, build, share, practise and apply.
- Run the job search as a funnel with referrals, tailored applications and tracking.
- Negotiate with market data and evaluate the whole offer.
- In the new role, learn the business, deliver early wins, communicate clearly and keep learning.
:::

:::quiz
Q: What should each week of the plan produce?
* At least one visible output (project progress, write-up or dashboard)
- Only reading
- Only certificates
- Nothing until week 12
> Evidence compounds.

Q: Which outreach is most effective?
* Personalised referral requests linking a relevant project
- Generic mass messages
- Applying to every job without reading it
- Waiting for recruiters
> Relevance wins.

Q: What matters when comparing offers?
* Full CTC breakdown plus learning, team and growth
- Only the fixed salary
- Only the company logo
- Only the job title
> Look at the whole package.
:::
